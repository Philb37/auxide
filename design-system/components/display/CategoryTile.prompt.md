Rounded square with one initial, coloured from a hue; used for categories, pockets and list leading slots.

```jsx
<CategoryTile hue={145} label="Groceries" size="lg" />
```

size: sm 28 · md 38 · lg 42 · xl 44. variant: tint (default) | white (on hero) | muted (archived). Also exports hueColors(h).
