# Logo / LogoMark

The Auxide mark: a gold coin (Au) with a rust crescent along its lower-right edge (oxide), "Au" in Fredoka on the face and a small rust dot at its top right. The wordmark colours "Au" gold and "xide" in ink.

- `<Logo size={36} wordSize={21}/>` sidebar; `size={44} wordSize={22}` welcome and sign-in.
- `dark` on ink backgrounds: lighter rust, brighter gold "Au", cream "xide".
- `<LogoMark/>` alone for app icons and avatars. Below 32px, drop the letters (coin and crescent only).
- Gold and rust are brand colours only. Do not use them for UI states, buttons or categories.
